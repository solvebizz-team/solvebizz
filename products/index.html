    Hotel software questions, answered

    Common questions about BitLegacy Hotels pricing, setup and features.

    How much does BitLegacy Hotels cost?

    Plans start at ₹999/month (Growth, up to 25 rooms), with Pro at ₹2,499/month and Pro+ at ₹4,999/month. Larger hotel groups get custom Business pricing. Every plan includes a 60-day free trial and there is no setup fee.

    Is there a free trial?

    Yes — a 60-day free trial with no credit card required. You get full access to your plan's features, and you can export your data and leave any time.

    Do I need technical skills to use it?

    No. BitLegacy Hotels is built for independent hotel owners and front-desk staff. Setup is guided and the dashboard is designed to be simple to learn.

    Does it handle GST-compliant billing?

    Yes. Invoices are generated with automatic GST slab calculation, CGST/SGST separation and your GSTIN — built specifically for Indian hotels.

    Can guests book directly without OTA commission?

    Yes. You get your own hotel website and a built-in booking engine, so guests book directly with zero OTA commission. Payments are collected via Razorpay (UPI, cards, net banking).

    Does it support multiple properties?

    Yes. The Pro+ and Business plans let you manage multiple hotels from one login, with per-property data separation and staff permissions.

    • 60-day free trial — no card, no setup fee
